# 01 – Vision & Pillars

## One-liner

A third-person open-world crime game where you claw your way out of debt in a
small Southeastern town that a big-box megastore is slowly swallowing, while the
city on the horizon pulls everything toward it.

## Tone

- **Southern Gothic meets satire.** Humid haze, kudzu, cicadas, red clay roads,
  church signs with puns, billboards for injury lawyers, a Dollar store on every
  corner.
- **Mature crime story** (violence, crime, language). Satire aimed at
  consumerism, debt, prosperity gospel, corporate "revitalization," and
  small-town power, not at the people living there.
- Funny on the surface, sincere underneath: the town is worth fighting for.

## Pillars

1. **A place that feels lived-in.** Cane Creek is small enough to learn by heart:
   you know the diner, the shortcut behind the mill, which deputy sits in the
   church lot. Density over size.
2. **Cars are the heart.** Pickups, muscle cars, backroads, dirt tracks, the
   bypass at night. Driving must feel good before anything else ships.
3. **Everything has a price tag.** Money is the main thing you play for and
   against: financing, credit score, repo men, hospital bills, loyalty points.
   Consumerism is a *mechanic*, not just a joke on a billboard.
4. **Escalation from town to city.** You start with a borrowed pickup and a
   pocketknife in a town of 4,000. You finish in the skyline you've been
   staring at the whole game.

## What makes it ours (not a clone)

We are working in the open-world crime genre, but every name, character, brand,
map, UI label and piece of art is original. Some examples of our own spin:

| Genre staple | Our version |
| --- | --- |
| Wanted stars | **Heat**, shown as sheriff badges; rural response is slow, town response is fast |
| Death screen | **CHECKED OUT**, then you get a hospital bill |
| Arrest screen | **PROCESSED**, then bail bondsman fees and a credit hit |
| Buying cars | **Financing with a credit score**; miss payments and a repo truck comes for your ride |
| Shops | **SaveMore Supercenter** loyalty program, Black Friday events, gig-app side jobs |
| Big city | Visible on the horizon from minute one; the interstate bridge into it is "closed for budget reasons" until Act 3 |

## Scope guardrails (v1)

In: single-player, browser first (desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox), one fixed protagonist, the Cane Creek region (about
9 km²), key interiors only (shops, safehouses, mission spaces).

Out (for now): multiplayer, mobile, aircraft, full city playable area (Act 3 / later
milestone), enterable interiors for every building, character creator.
