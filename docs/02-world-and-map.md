# 02 – World & Map

All names are working placeholders and fictional.

- **Cane Creek**: county seat, population about 4,000. The starting area.
- **Bascom County**: the rural county around it.
- **Port Meridian**: mid-size river city about 10 km northeast. A visible skyline
  backdrop at first; playable in Act 3.

## Scale

| Area | Size | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vertical slice | ~1 km² | Downtown Square + the Bypass |
| Cane Creek region (v1) | ~3 × 3 km | Town core is dense; the rest is forest, farms, backroads, lake |
| Port Meridian | ~2 × 2 km later | Until then it's a far-distance skyline impostor plus fog |

Rural land is cheap to build: procedural tree/scrub scatter plus a few hand-placed
landmarks. That's how a small team fills 9 km² without losing density in town.

## Districts

### Downtown Square
The heart of town. Brick storefronts around a courthouse with a clock tower.
- Bascom County Courthouse, Sheriff's Office and jail
- Dot's Diner (mission hub, save point), barbershop, hardware store, antique shops
- First Bascom Bank (Act 2 heist target)
- Two churches facing each other across the square
- The Rialto, a shuttered movie theater (hideout later)

### The Bypass (SR-9)
Four-lane commercial strip: the consumerism showcase.
- **SaveMore Supercenter**: giant parking lot, garden center, Black Friday riots
- **Big Earl's Auto**: car dealership with inflatable tube men; financing and repo jobs
- **Liberty Arms**: gun store and shooting range
- Fast-food row, gas stations, pawn shop, payday lender, vape shop, Dollar store
- Starlite Motel (cheap first safehouse option)

### Mill District
Closed textile mill, rail yard, warehouses, water tower. Industrial and run down.
- Cousin's chop shop (early mission hub)
- Gang and biker turf; good for chases and shootouts
- Freight trains pass through (later: trains as a system)

### Residential
- **Mill Village**: small shotgun houses, front porches, the protagonist's mom's house
- **Oak Hill**: Victorian homes, old money, the sheriff and the bank president live here
- **The Preserve at Cane Creek**: identical new-build subdivision with an HOA (satire target)
- **Shady Pines**: trailer park by the county road

### Outskirts (Bascom County)
- Pine forest, red clay backroads, chicken houses, farms, power lines everywhere
- Lake Tullis with marina and boat ramp
- Granite quarry, junkyard, dirt race track, county fairgrounds
- Megachurch on the highway ("Abundant Harvest")
- Fireworks superstore and truck stop at the I-48 interchange

### Port Meridian (backdrop → Act 3)
River city with a modest skyline: bank tower, stadium, cranes at the port. Seen
from the water tower, the interstate, and the lake at sunset. The I-48 bridge is
"closed for repairs" until the story opens it.

## Atmosphere

- **Time of day**: full day/night cycle. Golden-hour humidity haze, neon on the
  Bypass at night, the courthouse clock lit up.
- **Weather**: clear, hazy heat, summer thunderstorms, morning fog on the lake.
- **Ambient sound**: cicadas by day, crickets and frogs at night, distant trains,
  church bells Sunday morning, highway hum near the interstate.
- **Signature props**: water tower, kudzu-covered lots, church marquee signs,
  power poles and sagging lines, mailboxes on posts, porch swings, rusted pickups on blocks.
