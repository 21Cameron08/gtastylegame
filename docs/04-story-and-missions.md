# 04 – Story & Missions

All names are working placeholders.

## Premise

**Travis "Trip" Hollins** comes home to Cane Creek after four years in state
prison. His mother's house in Mill Village is three months from foreclosure
(a predatory refinance through First Bascom Bank), the mill that employed half
the town is closed, and **SaveMore** and its development partner **Crestline**
are buying up the county one "revitalization" at a time.

Trip needs money fast. Everyone in town has a way for him to make it, and every
one of them comes with strings.

**Theme**: everything is for sale, including you. What do you owe the place
that made you?

## Three-act outline

### Act 1: "Back Home" (Cane Creek)
Small-time hustle to save the house. Learn the town, the cars, the guns.
Introduces Bo, Big Earl, Granny Mae, Delia. Ends when Sheriff Pruitt arrests
Trip and offers to make it go away in exchange for dirty work.

### Act 2: "Buy Local" (Bascom County)
Trip works for the sheriff while playing every side: the bikers, the megachurch,
Crestline's land grab. He learns that the sheriff, the bank, and Crestline are
all working the same foreclosure scheme. Climax: the **First Bascom Bank heist**
during the county fair. The I-48 bridge reopens.

### Act 3: "The Big City" (Port Meridian)
The money trail leads to the city: Crestline HQ and the Meridian Outfit. Trip is
a small-town guy out of his depth in the skyline he's stared at all game.
Finale choice: burn it all down, or become the thing he hated.

## Mission design rules

- Every mission teaches or recombines something; no pure filler.
- Mid-mission checkpoints on anything longer than 3 minutes.
- Fail states should be clear and quick to retry.
- At least one optional path or approach per mission where it's cheap to offer
  (stealth vs. loud, backroad vs. highway).
- Payouts tie back into the economy: missions are the main income in Act 1,
  property and businesses take over in Act 2.
